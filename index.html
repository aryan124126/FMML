People and limits

A daily limit of 0 means no junk at all. Start with a limit you can actually hit, like 1 a day, and lower it once you've held a two-week streak. The weekly budget is the most you want to spend on junk in a week.

How the numbers work

  • Nutrition values are rough per-serving estimates for common Indian portions. Edit them when you log if you know better.
  • Daily maximums on the label: 2,000 kcal energy, 70 g fat, 50 g sugar (WHO says under 25 g is better), 2,000 mg sodium (WHO). The % shows how much of that came from junk alone.
  • About 7,700 kcal equals 1 kg of body fat. The fat figure on the Patterns tab converts junk calories to that.
  • A day with nothing logged counts as junk-free. The streak counts days at or under the daily limit, today included.
